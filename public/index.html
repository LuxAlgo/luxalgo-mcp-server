What it can do

Ask in plain words. Your AI picks the right tool and answers with links you can open.

The Library

800+ indicators and 800+ concepts from the largest indicator library in trading, each with its write-up: what it measures, the formula and how traders read it. Pine Script® source where it is published. Every indicator links straight to a Vela chart.

Try askingWhich Library indicators measure volatility? Open the first one on a chart.

Prop-firm challenges

Run a challenge thousands of times with your own stats or your real trades: the chance you pass, what it is likely to cost, and the rule that ends most attempts. Compare firms side by side. Simulations, not predictions or financial advice.

Try askingWith a 45% win rate at 2R, which $50K challenge am I most likely to pass?

Edge Stats

Session statistics such as gap fills, opening-range breakouts and day-of-week effects, each with its sample size and a 95% confidence interval, so you can see how often a setup actually worked.

Try askingHow often does Bitcoin close green on a Monday?

Trade Journal Sign in

Your LuxAlgo Trade Journal: performance, the P&L calendar, and breakdowns by weekday, time, symbol or tag. Log trades by hand, tag them and write notes, all from the chat.

Try askingWhere did I lose money last month? Tag my worst trade “chased”.
