MCP

The LuxAlgo ecosystem as an MCP server: the Library — the encyclopedia of trading & technical analysis; read-only broker access to your own accounts (local install only); Edge Stats — hosted session statistics with a sample size on every number; Market Trackers — the public record of US markets (congressional trades, insider filings, lobbying, contracts, patents and more) from CC0 dumps with a primary-source link on every row; the Monte Carlo challenge simulator; and the prop-firm directory (firms, challenges, live offers). Free, keyless, read-only.

Point any MCP client at https://mcp.luxalgo.com/mcp (streamable HTTP).
