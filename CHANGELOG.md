# Changelog

Notable changes to `@luxalgo/mcp`. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project follows [semver](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- `chart_open`, `chart_command` and `chart_sync` are marked `openWorldHint: true`: the chart streams candles from Hyperliquid and Binance, systems outside LuxAlgo. ChatGPT's automated review held the two model-facing chart tools for saying otherwise.

### Added
- Off by default: Paper Trading by LuxAlgo for the signed-in member, LuxAlgo's paper simulator with simulated money (never a broker, never live orders, never real money). Five protected tools, registered only with `LUXALGO_PAPER_TOOLS=on` or on a Vercel preview deployment and never on the Claude address (its 40 tools stay byte for byte the same): `paper_account` reads the member's paper accounts, positions, working orders and newest fills; `paper_propose_order`, `paper_close_position` and `paper_cancel_order` propose a change and return the exact order, its estimated price, value and margin, and a confirmation token, placing nothing; `paper_place_order` carries out a proposal the member approved, with that token and the order restated. The token works once, for that order and that member, for a few minutes; the app refuses anything else. Proposing tools are read-only; `paper_place_order` is marked destructive and closed-world, so hosts ask the user first, and every description tells the model to show the order and wait for the member's explicit yes. A new `paper-tools` smoke suite checks the anonymous refusals, the annotations and copy on the wire, the Claude address without them, and propose then place against a stand-in for the app (no token, a made-up one, a changed order and a second placement are refused).

- `LUXALGO_ALERT_TOOLS`: the alerts tools (`alerts_*`) now have their own switch on top of the account tools' one, and register only when both are on (`LUXALGO_ALERT_TOOLS=on`, or on a Vercel preview deployment). LuxAlgo Alerts are not open to every member yet, so the alerts tools stay off in production until they are. Watchlists, layouts and `chart_peek` are unchanged. The `surface` and `account-tools` smoke suites check that with the alert switch off no alerts tool is listed on any address.
- Off by default, with the account tools: `chart_peek`, a picture of the chart the signed-in member has open in Vela, for their AI assistant ("show me my chart"). A protected tool, registered only with `LUXALGO_ACCOUNT_TOOLS=on` or on a Vercel preview deployment and never on the Claude address (its 40 tools stay byte for byte the same). It asks the LuxAlgo app (`POST /api/agents/peek`, the member's own token), which gets the picture from the member's visible Vela tab; the result is MCP image content plus the market, timeframe and indicators in words and a Vela `chart_url`, and its structured content carries no image data. Only crypto charts fed by an exchange are pictured, by the app's rule; for any other market, with no open tab, a failed capture or a timeout, the tool says so plainly, and while the app has it switched off (404) it says it is not available yet. Optional `chart_index` picks one chart of a multi-chart layout. The `account-tools` smoke suite checks the anonymous refusal, its schema and copy, and each answer against the stand-in app.
- Off by default: the signed-in member's watchlists, saved Vela layouts and LuxAlgo Alerts, registered only with `LUXALGO_ACCOUNT_TOOLS=on` or on a Vercel preview deployment (the app routes they call are not in production yet), and never on the Claude address, whose tool list stays as it is under review. Eleven protected tools: `watchlists_list`, `watchlists_create`, `watchlists_add_symbols`, `watchlists_remove_symbols`, `layouts_list`, `alerts_list`, `alerts_create_price`, `alerts_create_custom`, `alerts_pause`, `alerts_resume`, `alerts_history`. Results are plain words with a Vela `chart_url` for the market (a layout's link opens it at `/chart/<id>`); prices appear only as the member's own levels and, in alert history, for exchange-fed crypto markets. A watchlist edit saves with the list's `updatedAt` and retries once when another device saved first. Alerts notify the member's in-app channel when they have one; with none, the alert is created without one and the result says it notifies no one. A Custom Alert is previewed first and its result says how often it would have fired. Plan limits are the app's answer, relayed as-is; nothing here deletes. A Price Alert made with an open chart's `chart_id` draws its level on it. A new `account-tools` smoke suite checks the anonymous refusals, the schemas and annotations on the wire, the Claude address without them, and the success paths against a stand-in for the app's routes (409 retry, 403 plan limits, no in-app channel, which prices history may show).

- Vela chart links (`chart_url`, "Open in Vela") on every result that names an indicator or a market: `library_search` indicator hits, `library_get_indicator`, `library_list_indicators` items, and a new `indicators` list on `library_get_concept` (the concept's implementations from one concept-filtered query, each with its link) open the indicator at `https://vela.luxalgo.com/chart?pineScriptCodeId=…`; `edge_report` and every `edge_symbols` entry open the hosted market at `?symbol=binance:BTCUSDT`, on 15-minute bars for intraday presets and daily bars for the rest. An indicator with no Vela script gets no link. Descriptions on those tools say what `chart_url` is. Not on the Claude address, whose tools and results stay byte for byte as they are while Anthropic reviews them; a `claude-surface` smoke suite holds that line (a hash of its tool list and the shape of its results) and checks the ChatGPT address carries the links.
- Spike, off by default: live Vela charts in the chat as an MCP App (`chart_open`, `chart_command`, app-only `chart_sync`, resource `ui://vela/chart`), registered only with `LUXALGO_CHART_TOOLS=on` or on a Vercel preview deployment. The view bundles Vela's open-source core (`@luxalgo/vela`) with its Hyperliquid and Binance providers into one HTML document (`npm run build:chart`); candles stream from the exchanges in the user's browser and no tool result carries prices. Edits travel through an in-process store that must move to a shared store before this ships.
  - Chart polish after a first run in ChatGPT: the tools are titled "Vela chart" and "Vela chart change" (ChatGPT prefixes a tool's title in its status line), with short status words while they run; picture in picture and full screen stay offered when the host does not list its display modes, fall back to ChatGPT's own bridge when the standard request is not granted, and say so calmly in the chart's header when neither works; a level's label sits inside the chart's left side instead of running off its right edge, follows panning and zooming, and shows prices with thousands separators (80,000).
- Live charts keep their edits in the LuxAlgo app's chart sessions when the app offers them, so `chart_command` and the view's `chart_sync` meet on any server instance, and the view's "Open in Vela" opens a Vela tab that follows the same chart. The model's `chart_id` carries the session's write key (`a.<id>.<key>`); the read key reaches the view only, in `chart_open`'s `_meta`. When the app answers 404 (its chart sessions are off) or cannot be reached, the chart stays in memory as before. The app client gains one narrow, capability-keyed path for those routes only (`docs/auth.md` §7a). `chart_command`'s and `chart_sync`'s `chart_id` accept longer ids, and `chart_sync` takes the view's `sync_key`. A new `chart` smoke suite checks the helpers, the memory fallback end to end, and the app backend against a stand-in for the app's routes.
- Live charts of a market fed by an exchange's public feed (Hyperliquid and Binance: every chart today) hand the model the numbers the user sees: the last price, the change since the day's open (00:00 UTC), the high and low of the visible range and each indicator's latest value ("RSI 38.2"), in the view's model context and in `chart_command`'s reply (`last_price`, `change_pct`, `visible_high`, `visible_low`, `indicator_values` in its `view`). Never bars. Whether a chart may do this is decided in one place from its provider (`numbersAllowedFor` in `tools/chart/market.ts`): any other source (a data vendor, futures, stocks) stays words only. The numbers stay out of the app's chart-session state, whose schema is words only; this server keeps the latest ones in its own memory for a short while.
  - `chart_command` takes `{op: "read"}`: it changes nothing and returns what the chart shows now, with those numbers. `{op: "read", image: true}` also has the view add a picture of the chart (JPEG, at most 1280 px wide and 300 KB) to the model context, where the chat app lists images among the content it takes there (`updateModelContext.image`, MCP Apps); the reply says when it cannot.

### Changed

- Market Trackers (`trackers_*`) are no longer on the ChatGPT address (`/mcp/openai`). They stay on the standard address, on stdio and on the Claude address, whose tool list is unchanged byte for byte. The `claude-surface` smoke suite checks the trackers tools per address.
- Live charts cost far fewer polls. The chart view checks `chart_sync` about every second only while something happens on the chart (for 30 seconds after it loads, applies an edit, is touched, or comes back into view, and while the model is at work on it), about every 6 seconds (with jitter) when it is idle, and not at all while the page is hidden or the chart has been out of view for 30 seconds; it starts again at once when shown. Each `chart_sync` now says when the view polls next (`next_poll_ms`, or `paused` on its last poll before stopping) and answers with the `next_poll_ms` the server wants: fast for 30 seconds after the model's last `chart_command` on that chart or while work is pending, slow otherwise. `chart_command` waits for the view's next poll when it is a few seconds off (at most 8 seconds instead of 5), and answers at once when it is further off ("applies them at its next check, within about N seconds") or the view stopped polling. Model-context updates are unchanged. The cadence lives in `tools/chart/polling.ts`, shared by the server and the view.
- The chart tools and the `ui://vela/chart` resource never register on the Claude address, with `LUXALGO_CHART_TOOLS=on` too, so its tool list stays byte for byte the one under review (the same pattern as the member account tools). The `chart` smoke suite checks it with the tools on, and the poll contract on an app session and on a chart in memory.
- README leads with Vela: a simpler cover (the Vela by LuxAlgo lockup and one line), Vela in your chat with the real chart capture, a compact Add it table, one table for everything else, and a short Build your own Vela. The full tool list moves to `docs/tools.md`; running it yourself, the environment variables and the architecture move to `docs/self-hosting.md`.
- The smoke suite's stdio server inherits the machine's outbound proxy and CA settings (and nothing else), so the live checks run behind a proxy.
- Faster answers. Indicator queries (`library_list_indicators`, `library_search`) are fetched anonymously (the app's public query route is the same for everyone), as card rows without the article body, cached for 5 minutes and retried in parallel when the app is slow to answer (one app instance in two took 4 to 6 seconds); the concepts list and the marketing-site markdown pages (`library_get_concept`, `library_get_family`) are cached too. `propfirms_simulate`, `propfirms_optimal_risk` and `propfirms_compare` results are cached per exact inputs and directory snapshot (they are deterministic under the seed); `propfirms_pass_rates` runs challenges whose encoded rules and fees are identical once. None of this changes an answer.
- Off the Claude address (the standard address, the ChatGPT address and stdio), answers are trimmed and say things in plain words. The Claude address keeps its reviewed tool definitions and results byte for byte while its directory review runs; a new `answers-surfaces` smoke suite (HTTP) pins its tool definitions and checks its results.
  - `propfirms_pass_rates` without `challengeId` returns a firm overview: one account size per rules program (closest to 100K, up to three programs), each a full run, with every other simulatable challenge listed by id (FTMO: 35 KB in 12 to 17 s before, 11 KB in about 2 s now).
  - `propfirms_list_simulatable` answers one line per firm (8 KB instead of 156 KB); its new optional `firmId` lists that firm's challenges exactly as before.
  - `propfirms_get` leaves out the FAQ (it restates the rules and payout policy; `propfirms_search` with `include: ['overview']` returns it), the firm fields repeated on every challenge, site plumbing (row id, logo, visibility) and bold markers, and adds the firm's page `url`. `library_get_family` no longer repeats the concept roster its page already lists, and gives `concept_count`.
  - Simulator result text states facts instead of instructing the assistant ("relay these to the user", "present both to the user"), names a challenge without repeating its firm ("FTMO - FTMO - …"), and says the fee in words: "Fee €540 per attempt, refunded on a pass; average cost across attempts €5" instead of "expected total cost: €5 (given funded: €5)".
  - `edge_report` refuses opening-gap presets (the `gaps` category and gap-fill outcomes) on symbols that trade around the clock, with the reason and the presets that apply: their sessions are calendar days that open a minute after the last one closed, so "gap fill 99.7%" measured nothing. `edge_presets` marks those presets with `not_applicable_to`.

### Fixed

- `library_get_source_code` said `chart_url` opens the indicator on Vela either way, but a complete source came without one. It now carries `chart_url` whenever the indicator has a Vela script, complete, partial or not served (the Claude address keeps today's result and description until its review is done).
- `propfirms_search_challenges` finds challenges for natural queries such as "FTMO 100K", "FTMO 2-step 100k", "ftmo 100000" or "the5ers 2 step", which matched nothing because the app's `text` filter is a plain substring match. When a text search comes back empty, the account size (100k, 12.5K, 1M, 100000), step count (2-step, two phase, instant) and firm in the text are mapped onto the existing structured filters (`accountSizeMin`/`accountSizeMax`, `steps`, `propfirmId`, `challengeName`), so the rows are exactly what those filters return, and the result says how it read the query (`interpretedAs`). Searches that already found rows answer exactly as before. When nothing matches, the result says why: a firm the directory does not list (with the firms it does), or the steps and account sizes the firm offers.
- `propfirmId` and `challengeId` accept a single id (`"ftmo"`) as well as a list (`["ftmo"]`) on `propfirms_search`, `propfirms_search_challenges` and `propfirms_search_offers`. The schema only widens: every call valid before stays valid.
- Both apply on `/mcp`, `/mcp/openai` and stdio. The Claude host keeps its reviewed definitions and results byte for byte while its directory review is open; a `claude-freeze` smoke suite checks them against `test/smoke/fixtures/claude-propfirms-tools.json`.

It opens the indicator on Vela, LuxAlgo's charts, at `https://vela.luxalgo.com/chart?pineScriptCodeId=…`; `LUXALGO_CHART_ORIGIN` points it at a non-production Vela. `quant_url` is unchanged for existing clients, and the app redirects it to the same chart.

A `surface` smoke check asserts it.
- **Claude-only host** `https://claude.mcp.luxalgo.com/mcp`, the workaround for [claude-ai-mcp#1013](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-ai-mcp/issues/1013) (claude.ai starts OAuth at connect time for any server whose well-known PRM it finds). Same server and tools; on that host the well-known PRM paths answer 404, the metadata lives at `/auth/prm` (clients learn it from the 401's `resource_metadata`), `/register` stays 404 and `tools/list` carries no `securitySchemes` — so the anonymous surface looks authless and claude.ai signs users in only when a protected tool is called. It is its own resource identifier (token audience), the standard host's with `claude.` in front; the app must list it as a resource (`getLuxalgoClaudeMcpServerResource`). `https://mcp.luxalgo.com/mcp` stays standard RFC 9728 for every other client. The host is picked per request (`src/auth/surface.ts`); a new `discovery` smoke suite checks both. Remove once #1013 is fixed.

### Changed

- On the ChatGPT and Claude addresses, tools no longer carry the analytics `context` argument, and the simulator's descriptions state what results carry instead of instructing the model ("always surface…", "relay…"), per Claude's directory policy. `/mcp` is unchanged.

- README and the landing page describe the open-source library as the open-source core of Vela by LuxAlgo ("Vela for Developers" stays only as a label), linking [velacharts.dev](https://velacharts.dev) instead of the old luxalgo.com/vela page, and point to Vela, LuxAlgo's charts, at [vela.luxalgo.com/chart](https://vela.luxalgo.com/chart).

### Removed

- `library_list_tags`, and the `tags` filter and per-indicator `tags` field of `library_list_indicators`. The Library's tags are retired: it is organized by family and concept only, so browse with `library_list_families` and `library_list_concepts`, or filter `library_list_indicators` by `family` or `concept`.

Hiding the well-known PRM for the whole origin fixed Claude but broke every standard client (ChatGPT's app submission: "couldn't detect OAuth metadata"); the Claude-only host above does the same thing without touching the standard one. `securitySchemes` was ruled out as the trigger and is now always on for the standard host.

## [1.5.0] - 2026-09-24

### Added

- **Trade Journal tools** (`journal_*`, thirteen, all sign-in required): the signed-in user's journal in the LuxAlgo app. Reads — `journal_list_accounts` (accounts + the journal timezone; the first call, since every `accounts` filter takes its ids), `journal_overview` (metrics, Edge Score, daily P&L, equity curve, open positions, accounts and settings for a window, with an optional previous-window comparison), `journal_calendar`, `journal_breakdown` (P&L by weekday, time of day, hold time, symbol, side, size, tag, rating, asset class), `journal_list_trades` (keyset-paginated summaries with account / day-window / symbol / direction / status / tag filters), `journal_get_trade` (fills, per-exit P&L, all annotations), `journal_get_day`, `journal_list_tags` (the user's tag / mistake / playbook vocabulary with usage counts), `journal_search_notes` (day notes and trade notes as one paginated, text-searchable stream). Writes — `journal_add_trade` (fills into a manual or import account; returns the resulting trades), `journal_update_trade` (notes, tags and mistakes with replace or add/remove forms, playbook, rating, stop loss, profit target, reviewed), `journal_write_note`, `journal_update_note`. Everything runs as the signed-in user against the app's `/api/journal/*` routes; the app owns validation and entitlements as for every other tool.
- `journal_list_trades` takes `sort` (`openedAt` default, `closedAt`, `netPnl`, `grossPnl`, `durationMs`, `quantity`, `symbol`, `rating` — named after the summary fields) and `order` (`desc` default; `symbol` defaults to `asc`), mirroring the app's `GET /api/journal/trades`. Trades without the sort value (open trades for `closedAt`/`durationMs`, unrated for `rating`) come last either way; a cursor only pages within the same sort and order.
- `appSend()` in `src/platform/app-client.ts`: the first write path to the app (POST / PATCH / PUT / DELETE), sharing `appGet`'s envelope handling and 401/403 mapping. It has no anonymous form — with no ambient token it throws `AppAuthError` before any request goes out, so a caller without a sign-in meets the challenge, not a half-made write.
- `LUXALGO_SECURITY_SCHEMES=off` removes the per-tool `securitySchemes` hint from `tools/list`. Added to test whether Claude.ai's connector reads that field when it decides to sign users in at connect time; the 401 challenge on protected calls is unchanged. (Ruled out: production ran with it off and Claude.ai still signed users in at connect — the well-known PRM probe is the trigger; tracked as [claude-ai-mcp#1013](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-ai-mcp/issues/1013).)
- `LUXALGO_REACTIVE_AUTH_ONLY=on` (default off) makes auth discovery purely reactive while [claude-ai-mcp#1013](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-ai-mcp/issues/1013) is open: the two well-known PRM paths answer 404 and the metadata document moves to `/auth/prm` (clients learn it from the 401's `resource_metadata`), leaving the anonymous origin indistinguishable from an authless server's — `/register` included, which stays 404: the official `modelcontextprotocol/ext-apps` lazy-auth example (`REACTIVE_AUTH_ONLY`, which this ports) answers it with an RFC 7591 "not supported" error, but claude.ai surfaces that 400 as a fatal registration failure instead of connecting without auth. Deliberately non-RFC 9728 while on — see `docs/auth.md` §5 for what to verify after enabling.
- `Dockerfile` and `.dockerignore`: an image that runs the stdio server (`node dist/index.js`), for MCP directories such as Glama that build the repository and introspect the running server.
- The Release workflow publishes `server.json` to the official MCP Registry (`io.github.LuxAlgo/luxalgo-mcp-server`) after the npm publish, with a pinned `mcp-publisher` and GitHub OIDC (no secret). It can also be started by hand from main (`workflow_dispatch`), and it skips `npm publish` when the version is already on npm, so a re-run still reaches the registry step.

### Changed

- Every tool now declares MCP `annotations` (`readOnlyHint`, `openWorldHint`; the journal writes already carried `destructiveHint`) — the journal and account domains had them, the read-only domains (library, propfirms, trackers, edge, broker) did not, and the MCP directory's review criteria ask for them on every tool.
- App API errors other than 401/403 now carry the app's own message when it sent one (`"Trade not found"`, `"Nothing to update"`, `"Account not found"`) instead of the generic `LuxAlgo API <status> for <path>`; the status is unchanged and `isNotFound()` still works. The journal routes answer 400/404 with plain-language reasons an agent needs to recover, and the other domains gain the same.
- The smoke surface suite derives its protected-tool assertions from one `PROTECTED` list (`test/smoke/surface.mjs`) instead of naming `luxalgo_account`; a new `journal` suite checks that reads and writes alike are refused anonymously (401 hosted, in-band on stdio) and that the input schemas are on the wire as designed. The stdio server under test now runs with `LUXALGO_MCP_AUTH_FILE` pointed at an absent file, so a developer's own `luxalgo-mcp login` can no longer turn the suite's "anonymous" protected calls — one of which is a write — into real requests against their account.
- The anonymous 401 on a protected tool now carries `error="invalid_token", error_description="Authentication required for this tool"` and lists the challenge parameters in the order Anthropic's lazy-authentication guide shows — the same signal, byte-compatible with their sample. The smoke suite asserts the wire response (status + header) instead of the SDK client's error text.

- README: Claude (web/desktop/mobile) install instructions. Claude.ai signs in at connect time for any server with discoverable OAuth metadata — it fetches the well-known PRM itself after a successful anonymous handshake, under either *Authentication* setting — so the docs now say so instead of promising lazy auth there. `docs/auth.md` records the observed request sequence so nobody treats the connect-time popup as a server bug.
- `server.json`: a registry description within the schema's 100-character limit (the previous 405-character one failed validation), a `title`, and `websiteUrl` [luxalgo.com/mcp](https://www.luxalgo.com/mcp). `package.json` gains the same `homepage` and a description that includes Edge Stats and the Trade Journal.
- README: an H1 and a link to [luxalgo.com/mcp](https://www.luxalgo.com/mcp).

## [1.4.1]

### Changed

- Source layout reorganised by concern (no change to tool names, schemas, outputs, the wire protocol, or the `luxalgo-mcp` binary). `src/lib/` is gone: each tool domain now lives in `src/tools/<domain>/` behind a declarative `ToolModule` (`index.ts`: tool names, which are protected, whether the module is local-only), listed once in `src/server/manifest.ts`; registration asserts every module registers exactly the tools it declares, and the HTTP gate's protected-tool set and the `securitySchemes` hint are derived from the manifest instead of being maintained by hand. OAuth moved to `src/auth/` (the former `protected-tools.ts` split into `runtime`, `errors`, `instrument`, `protected-tool`), the app HTTP client to `src/platform/app-client.ts` with per-domain endpoint wrappers next to their tools, and the entries to `src/entries/` (`stdio`, `node-http`, `hosted`). The 900-line simulator file is now `tools/propfirms/simulation/{package-tools,shared,pass-rates,validate-strategy}.ts`; the twelve copies of `json` / `toolError` / `compact` are one `tools/_shared/`.
- `npm run start:http` now runs `dist/entries/node-http.js`; `dist/http.js` remains as a shim so existing `node dist/http.js` process managers keep working.
- Tests moved from `scripts/` to `test/`; the smoke suite is split per domain under `test/smoke/` (run a subset with `node test/smoke.mjs --only library,edge`) and now also asserts that the advertised server version matches `package.json`.
- The shared `guarded()` result helper lets the app's 401/403 (`AppAuthError` / `AppPermissionError`) propagate to the auth wrapper instead of flattening them into a generic tool error, so a tool using it still yields the sign-in challenge or the upgrade message.

### Added

- `AGENTS.md` (rules and the add-a-tool recipe for AI coding agents; `CLAUDE.md` imports it for Claude Code) and `docs/auth.md` (how OAuth, token forwarding and the public/protected distinction work, and what tool code may and may not do).

## [1.4.0]

### Changed

- MCP SDK v2 and the 2026-07-28 protocol: the server now runs on `@modelcontextprotocol/server` 2 (replacing `@modelcontextprotocol/sdk` 1.x) and zod 4. All three entries serve the stateless 2026-07-28 protocol and still answer 2025-era clients (current Cursor builds included) through the SDK's per-request legacy fallback — stdio via `serveStdio`, the plain-Node HTTP entry via `createMcpHandler` + `@modelcontextprotocol/node`, and the Vercel entry via `mcp-handler` 2. Tool names, input schemas, and outputs are unchanged; `POST /mcp` and `/health` are unchanged.
- The Vercel entry now advertises the same `serverInfo` (`luxalgo` / package version) as the stdio and plain-Node entries instead of `mcp-handler`'s default.
- `@luxalgo/prop-firm-sim-mcp` 1.3.0: the simulator tools are re-exported from its new `/tools` and `/directory` package entry points instead of deep `dist/` imports, and share its zod 4 schemas directly (no more zod 3/4 split). ### Added

- OAuth 2.1 sign-in with a LuxAlgo account, with [app.luxalgo.com](https://app.luxalgo.com) as the authorization server and this server as an RFC 9728 protected resource (`https://mcp.luxalgo.com/mcp`). Public tools keep working anonymously; protected tools need a signed-in user. This server makes no entitlement decisions of its own: once the caller has a token, every request a tool makes to the app carries it as a bearer, and the app resolves the user and their plan exactly as it does for a browser session — answering `401` (→ sign-in challenge) or `403` with the failing permission (→ "your plan does not include this") when appropriate.
  - Hosted entries (Vercel and plain Node) serve `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource` (root and path-inserted forms), verify bearer tokens offline against the app's JWKS (issuer, audience, expiry; DPoP-bound tokens are refused since the resource advertises no DPoP support), and refuse an anonymous call to a protected tool with `401` + `WWW-Authenticate` pointing at the metadata — "lazy" auth, so `initialize` and `tools/list` never prompt. `LUXALGO_AUTH_CHALLENGE=result` switches the anonymous-protected-call case to an in-band tool error carrying `_meta["mcp/www_authenticate"]`.
  - `tools/list` declares a per-tool `securitySchemes` (`noauth` / `oauth2` + scopes), the extension ChatGPT keys its per-tool linking on; other clients ignore it.
  - Local (stdio) entry: `npx -y @luxalgo/mcp login` / `logout` / `status`. The local server is itself the OAuth client (authorization code + PKCE, loopback redirect, refresh tokens; identified by the Client ID Metadata Document at `/oauth/client.json`, or DCR against a non-https resource). Tokens are stored owner-only under the user's config dir (`LUXALGO_MCP_AUTH_FILE` to override) and refreshed silently. With a client that supports URL-mode elicitation (MCP 2026-07-28), the first protected call signs in in-session instead.
  - First protected tool: `luxalgo_account` — plan tier, entitlements and profile basics from the app's `/api/account/me`, on every entry.
- New runtime dependencies: `jose` (JWT verification) and `@modelcontextprotocol/client` (the SDK's OAuth client, previously dev-only).
- Env: `MCP_RESOURCE` (resource identifier / token audience, default `https://mcp.luxalgo.com/mcp`); the issuer follows the existing `LUXALGO_APP_ORIGIN`.
- The smoke suite (`npm test`, `npm run test:http`) now also checks the advertised `securitySchemes` on the wire and the unauthenticated behaviour of `luxalgo_account` on both transports.
- Market Trackers tools: `trackers_datasets`, `trackers_query`, `trackers_latest`, `trackers_ticker`. The public record of US markets — congressional trades, insider (Forms 3/4/5) transactions, 13F holdings, federal contracts and grants, lobbying filings, FINRA short-sale volume, granted patents, clinical trials, FDA drug events, CFTC positioning, federal bills, FEC campaign finance, hearing transcripts, Federal Reserve communications, committee assignments, Wikipedia pageviews — read directly from the CC0 dumps published by [LuxAlgo/market-trackers](https://github.com/LuxAlgo/market-trackers) (live tree plus deep-history archive releases), streamed shard by shard under a per-call byte budget. Keyless; every row carries a primary-source `provenance.sourceUrl`.
- Edge Stats tools (all entries, hosted included): `edge_symbols`, `edge_presets`, `edge_report`. Hosted session statistics from the open-source [edge-stats](https://github.com/LuxAlgo/edge-stats) engine, precomputed nightly and served verbatim in the engine's honesty envelope (N, Wilson 95% CI, guards, stability split, per-year counts, disclaimer). Keyless; reads only public release artifacts containing derived statistics, never raw bars.
